1 · What do you know about AI?

Before reading, think of three places where you have seen or used AI. AI is not only a chatbot: recommendation systems, image recognition, navigation, voice assistants and automated classification can also use AI.

2 · Learn the Four Key Ideas

🤖
Artificial Intelligence

Technology designed to perform tasks that can involve recognition, prediction, classification, generation or decision support.

🧠
Machine Learning

A way of building AI systems that learn useful relationships or patterns from examples and data.

🔎
Pattern

A repeated characteristic, relationship or behavior that can be observed in data.

📈
Trend

A general direction or tendency visible across data, often as values increase, decrease or behave consistently.

DATA→AI ANALYSIS→PATTERN→TREND→VERIFY→DECISION

📓 Concept Check

In your notebook, write a short definition in your own words for: Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Pattern and Trend. Then write three examples of AI you see in daily life.

3 · AI in Daily Life

Recommendations

Music, videos or products can be suggested from patterns in previous interactions.

Classification

A system can classify images, messages or other inputs into categories.

Data Analysis

AI assistants can help summarize a dataset, compare values and propose patterns to investigate.

4 · Simple AI Demonstration

Two ways AI can work with patterns

Classification Demo

With a tool such as Teachable Machine, examples are grouped into classes. The model uses the examples to classify a new input.

Flow: Examples → Training → New input → Prediction

AI Assistant Data Analysis

An AI assistant can receive structured data and help identify high values, low values, repeated relationships and possible trends. The result must be checked against the original data.

9 · Human vs. AI Verification

🧠 Human responsibility

Read the original data, check calculations, question conclusions, identify missing information and decide whether a finding is justified.

🤖 AI support

Help summarize, compare, classify, propose patterns, explain findings and suggest possible actions for human evaluation.
