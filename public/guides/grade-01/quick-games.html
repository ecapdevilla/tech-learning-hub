1st Grade — Robot Rescue

A playful lesson on sequencing and algorithms. Students program a robot with simple steps to rescue the lost teddy.

Goal

Program the robot by choosing a sequence of moves so it reaches the teddy without falling into holes. Each correct rescue gives the team a point.

Teacher notes
  • Duration: 20–30 minutes
  • Group size: pairs
  • Learning goal: sequencing, prediction, debugging

How to play

  1. Choose a team and open the grid.
  2. Use the arrow buttons to add up to 6 steps into your sequence.
  3. Press Run to simulate the robot. If it reaches the teddy, you score.
  4. Discuss why a sequence failed and try again (debugging).
Use these activities to lead a 20–30 minute playful lesson. Adapt difficulty by increasing sequence length.
