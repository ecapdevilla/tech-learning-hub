1st Grade — Quick Game Activities

Short, playful activities you can run in class to teach algorithms, sequences, and teamwork. All content in English.

Warm-up: Dance Sequence

Students create a 4-step dance sequence (left, right, jump, clap). Call out the sequence and have pairs perform it.

Try: Random Sequence

Press start to generate a sequence to perform.

Team Relay

Split the class in teams; each student adds one step to the sequence.

Core: Treasure Hunt (Algorithmic thinking)

Create a grid on the board and give students simple instructions to reach the treasure (move 2 steps right, 1 up...).

Use these activities to lead a 20–30 minute playful lesson. Adapt difficulty by increasing sequence length.
