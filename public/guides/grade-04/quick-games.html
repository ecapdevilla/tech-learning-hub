4th Grade — Color Capture

Lesson focus: Conditionals & Sorting

A competitive game where students write simple rules to collect colored tokens and score points. Encourages conditionals and comparisons.

Teacher guide
  • Duration: 30–40 minutes
  • Skills: conditionals, sorting, algorithms
  • Materials: colored cards or tokens
Select rule:

How to play

  1. Choose a rule from the dropdown (teacher can change per round).
  2. Students work in teams to write the rule in plain language and predict which tokens will be collected.
  3. Press Start round — tokens are revealed; teams check predictions and score points.
