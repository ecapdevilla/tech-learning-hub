Algorithm Adventure

Learn how algorithms work by putting steps in order, guiding a robot, finding bugs and solving simple sequence challenges.

🎯 Learning Objective

Recognize, order and create simple sequences of instructions to solve everyday and digital problems.

Algorithms and Sequences

Teacher tip: project the game on the classroom screen. Students can solve each challenge together, in teams, or take turns at the computer.
